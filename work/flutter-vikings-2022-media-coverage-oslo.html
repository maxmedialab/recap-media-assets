About the event

Two days of talks in three parallel rooms

Flutter Vikings brought developers, tech leads and open-source contributors from across Europe and beyond to Oslo for two days of Flutter and Dart talks.

Talks, workshops and community sessions ran in three parallel rooms at the Felix Conference Center in Aker Brygge. People who could not travel followed the livestream, and the sessions went up on YouTube afterwards.

Our role

Livestreamed, recorded and photographed

  • We livestreamed all three parallel rooms at the same time, on a multi-camera setup.

  • We recorded the talks and edited them for YouTube: about 20 hours of footage became 43 published videos.

  • We photographed the whole venue: speakers, the audience, the venue itself and the moments between sessions.

  • We also filmed extra footage around the venue for social media and promotion.

This is what our conference photo and video coverage looks like in practice, with event photography and event video under one booking. More projects on our work page.
