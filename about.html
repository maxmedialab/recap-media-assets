The founder

Hey, I'm Maks

Your event took months of planning, coordination, and budget to pull together. The content that comes out of it should reflect that. I started Recap Media to make sure it does, so that what you walk away with isn't just photos and footage, but content your team can actually use.

I've been working as a freelance videographer and photographer since 2018, covering everything from 20-person boardroom sessions to conferences with several hundred attendees. Along the way I realised that strategy, more than quality, decides whether footage gets used or archived.

Knowing what your marketing team needs before you show up. Shooting for the platforms the content is going to live on. Delivering while the event is still fresh in people's minds.

Being an outsider in Oslo (I came from Poland and built a life here) means I naturally bridge the international and the local. I work comfortably with English-speaking agencies organising events in Norway, and I'm building deep knowledge of Oslo's venues, light, and logistics with every project.

Outside of work you'll find me running, lifting, or somewhere in the Norwegian outdoors. If you've heard of Norseman: not done yet, but definitely on the list.

If you've got an event coming up, I'd love to hear about it.

Why we exist

Events deserve better content

Events can cost hundreds of thousands in venue, catering, and logistics, yet the media coverage gets treated as an afterthought. A phone camera on the day, folders of unedited files that never get used, or a highlight video that arrives three weeks later, when the LinkedIn post window has already closed and the sponsor report is filed.

We started Recap Media because we believed events deserved better than that.

    Photos in up to 7 business days, and video on the timeline we agree before the event, usually within 14 business days.

  3. On your channels

    Ready to publish

    Formatted and sized for the platforms where your audience actually is.

How a booking works

From the first call to the final delivery

Simple to book, planned around your goals, and you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We find out what the photos and video are for, and what success looks like for you.

    Your partTell us about the event.

  2. 02

    Options on a strategy call

    You get three coverage options, explained on a strategy call.

    Your partBring whoever decides and pick what fits.

  3. 03

    Agreement and kickoff

    Everything is agreed in writing. At kickoff we go through speakers, names and key moments.

    Your partSign and share your programme.

  4. 04

    Planning to the day

    We make a shot list from your programme and check the venue before the day.

    Your partTell us when plans change.

  5. 05

    Event day

    One contact on site, and a crew sized to what we agreed.

    Your partHost your event.

  6. 06

    Delivery and review

    Photos in up to 7 business days. Video on the timeline we agree before the event, usually within 14 business days, with two revision rounds.

    Your partName one person to collect feedback.

Questions about us

Maks Strzepka founded Recap Media after years of freelance event work across Europe. The company grew from a simple conviction: live events deserve better coverage than a last-minute phone call to a photographer. One dedicated team, one brief, better results.
Maks plans and coordinates every booking, and usually leads the coverage on site as photographer or videographer. Depending on the scope, trusted Oslo-based crew members, vetted through past projects, join him or cover the event from the same plan.
We shoot with mirrorless camera systems (Sony) for both photo and video, paired with professional audio capture. The specific kit depends on the event; we adapt rather than bringing a fixed setup.
