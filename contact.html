FAQ

Before you get in touch

Every enquiry gets a real reply within one business day, usually faster. A message sent on Friday afternoon gets its reply on Monday at the latest.
Our primary service area is Oslo and surroundings, including Sandvika, Fornebu, Drammen, Lillestrøm, Jessheim, Holmestrand, and Moss. We also travel for larger conferences across Norway and internationally. Travel costs are agreed upfront and included in the quote.
Helpful to know: the event date and location, approximate number of attendees, the type of event (conference, launch, trade show), what deliverables you need (photos, video, both), and any key moments you want captured. Don't worry if you don't have all of this; we'll ask the right questions on the discovery call.
It depends on the event, so there is no fixed menu. After the discovery call you get coverage options built around your programme. For a feel of scale, see our example coverage options.
