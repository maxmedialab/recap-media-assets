What full production includes

  • Every event day on site

    Full days of photo and video coverage. The number of days is set in your options.

  • A crew sized to what we agree

    Maks Strzepka leads the project on site. One booking, one point of contact, one plan.

  • A hero film

    The story of the whole event in one film, typically around 2 minutes, depending on the event.

  • A faster highlight cut

    A short cut for social media while the event is still fresh.

  • Social clips

    Short vertical clips cut from the same footage, ready to post.

  • Press-ready photos after each day

    A press-ready selection within 24 hours of each event day, for press, social and sponsor updates.

  • The full photo gallery

    Edited and in high resolution, in up to 7 business days.

  • Two revision rounds on the video

    On a review link where you comment at the exact moment. The details are set out in the agreement.

Add-ons

Common additions

These can be added to this scope, and each one is scoped into the same quote.

  • Basic livestream

    Single-camera livestream to YouTube, Vimeo or your own platform, on request and scoped per event. Whether it fits yours depends on the setup, so ask us and we'll give you a concrete answer.

  • Sponsor clips

    Short showcase clips for each sponsor or exhibitor, to add to what you offer your sponsors.

  • Session recordings

    Keynotes and panels recorded in full, for members, sponsors or your archive.

  • Branded graphics

    Title cards, lower thirds and graphics in your event's branding, across all the films.

  • Raw footage

    Not included by default. It can be purchased separately, best agreed in advance.
