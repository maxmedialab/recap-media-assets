The process

How It Works

01

Discovery call

We learn about your event, audience, and goals. You tell us what content you need and how you'll use it. The tailored quote follows this brief.

02

Pre-event brief

We create a detailed shot list, timeline, and coverage plan. You approve it before event day.

03

Event day(s)

We handle everything on-site. Daily selects delivered overnight. You focus on your event.

04

Edit & deliver

Photos in up to 7 business days, video in up to 14 business days. 2 revision rounds included. You get publish-ready content.

Add-ons

Common Additions

Every event is different. These are the additions clients ask about most often, and each one is scoped into the same tailored quote:

Basic livestream Single-camera livestream to YouTube, Vimeo or your own platform, on request and scoped per event. Whether it fits yours depends on the setup, so ask us and we'll give you a concrete answer.
Sponsor mini-clips 15-30 second showcase clips per sponsor or exhibitor, a high-value addition to what you offer sponsors.
Photo gallery hosting An online gallery where attendees can browse and download their own photos for 30 days after the event.
