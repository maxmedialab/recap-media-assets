The process

How It Works

01

Discovery call

We learn about your event, audience, and goals. You tell us what content you need and how you'll use it. The tailored quote follows this brief.

02

Pre-event brief

We create a detailed shot list, timeline, and coverage plan. You approve it before event day.

03

Event day

We handle everything on-site. You focus on your event, we focus on capturing it.

04

Edit & deliver

Photos delivered in up to 7 business days. 2 revision rounds included. You get publish-ready content.
