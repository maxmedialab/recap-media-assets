02 Before you get a number

How the Quote Is Put Together

Tell us the date, the venue, the programme and what the material has to do afterwards. We scope the coverage against that brief and send one tailored quote covering crew, deliverables, turnaround and any add-ons, such as express delivery, extra social cuts, sponsor clips or on-site interviews.

Maks Strzepka leads every shoot, with a vetted crew sized to the event. One booking, one point of contact, from the brief to delivery.

Not sure which scope fits?

Most events we cover run between 20 and 500 attendees, and they sit all along this scale. Send the brief and we will tell you which scope is closest, including when a smaller one is enough.
