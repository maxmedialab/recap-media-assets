01 What we produce

Event Video That Keeps Working After the Day

One crew, one brief, and a set of edits sized for the places your team actually publishes: the website, LinkedIn, the newsletter and the internal archive.

From live coverage to long-form sessions

Keynotes, panels and presentations recorded in full and delivered ready to publish or share internally, alongside the atmosphere footage the recap edit is built from.

Recap films, aftermovies and highlight reels

A branded narrative edit of the event, typically around 2 minutes, depending on the event type, length and what the film needs to show. Shorter highlight cuts of 30–90 seconds come from the same material.

Social clips and vertical edits from one event

Speaker moments cut for LinkedIn and Instagram in both 16:9 and 9:16, subtitled, and sized so your social team can schedule them for weeks rather than post once.

Vox pops and speaker testimonials

Attendee vox pops and planned speaker or sponsor testimonials, recorded between sessions with a simple on-site interview setup.

02 Social-first video

One Filming Day,
Every Format

Vertical clips for LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok

Every filming day produces a 16:9 master edit and 9:16 vertical clips from the same material. Your social team gets platform-ready video, with no awkward crops and no separate shoot.

Session recordings and full presentations

The same day also produces the long-form archive: complete talks your members, sponsors and internal teams can watch later, and the source material every shorter cut is pulled from.

06 How it works

How We Plan Your Event Video

Step 1

Pre-event brief and shot list

We agree deliverables, key moments, formats and deadlines before the day, and turn your programme into a shot list. You get one contact from start to finish.

Step 2

Founder-led crew on site

Maks Strzepka is on the floor, not a subcontractor you have not met. We film discreetly around your programme (sessions, atmosphere, interviews) without staging the event.

Step 3

Editing and revisions

Editing, colour and sound, plus your branding: logos, lower-thirds and subtitles in Norwegian or English. Two revision rounds on video edits are included, with the details set out in the agreement.

Step 4

Delivery timelines

High-resolution MP4s via private link, in up to 14 business days from the event. If a specific deadline matters, say so in the brief and you get a straight answer on what is realistic.

07 What you get

Deliverables and Usage Rights

Recap films and highlight cuts

The branded narrative edit, typically around 2 minutes, depending on the event type, length and what the film needs to show, plus shorter cuts for feeds and for next year's promotion.

Full presentations and session archives

Complete talks and panels as separate files, named and ordered so they can go straight onto your site, your member portal or your internal drive.

Social clips, captions and subtitles

Vertical and square versions, with subtitles in Norwegian or English so the clips still land when they play without sound.

Commercial rights and raw footage

Full commercial usage rights to every delivered video: web, social, internal and marketing use, with no licensing fees. Raw footage is not part of the standard delivery; it can be purchased separately and is best discussed in advance.
