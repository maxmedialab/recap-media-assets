How a booking works

From the first call to the final film

Simple to book, planned around your goals, and you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We find out what the videos are for, and what success looks like for you.

    Your partTell us about the event.

  2. 02

    Options on a strategy call

    You get three coverage options, explained on a strategy call.

    Your partBring whoever decides and pick what fits.

  3. 03

    Agreement and kickoff

    Everything is agreed in writing. At kickoff we go through speakers, interviews and key moments.

    Your partSign and share your programme.

  4. 04

    Planning to the day

    We build a shot list from your programme and check the venue before the day.

    Your partTell us when plans change.

  5. 05

    Event day

    One contact on site, and a crew sized to what we agreed.

    Your partHost your event.

  6. 06

    Review and delivery

    Video in up to 14 business days, with two revision rounds.

    Your partName one person to collect feedback.

Consent and privacy

How we support your event's consent process

  • Your process, followed

    Tell us how your attendees are informed about filming, and the crew follows your process on the day.

  • Opt-out signals honoured

    Wristbands, lanyards or stickers: brief us and the whole crew watches for them. Anyone who asks to be left out is left out.

  • You control what gets used

    Every edit comes to you first on a private review link, and a shot comes out if someone raises a concern.
