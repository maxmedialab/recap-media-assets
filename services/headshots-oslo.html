How a booking works

From the first call to the final portraits

Simple to book, little to organise, and you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Tell us the basics

    How many people, roughly when and where. A quick call settles the details, and email works too.

    Your partA rough headcount is enough to start.

  2. 02

    Quote and the look

    You get a quote for your headcount, and we agree the look with you: background, framing and how many finals each person gets. Your existing guidelines work too.

    Your partSend earlier portraits or brand guidelines if you have them.

  3. 03

    Shoot day

    We bring the lights and set up in whatever space your office has free. Each person is done in a few minutes and goes straight back to work.

    Your partA room or a bit of free space, and a heads-up to the team.

  4. 04

    Picks and delivery

    Within 3 business days you get a private selection link. Each person picks their favourites, and the edited finals follow within 7 business days of the picks.

    Your partRemind people of the date to pick by.
