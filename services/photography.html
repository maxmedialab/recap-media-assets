01 What we photograph

B2B Events Across Oslo and Beyond

From 20-person seminars to multi-track conferences. If it has a programme, speakers, or a stand — we photograph it.

Conferences & seminars

Keynotes, panels, breakout sessions, audience reactions. We work discreetly — no interrupted talks, no blocked sightlines — and cover speakers in a way their own teams want to repost.

Trade fairs & exhibitions

Stand photography for exhibitors at NOVA Spektrum in Lillestrøm and trade fairs across Oslo and beyond. Staff at work, visitor traffic, product details — material your sales team can use all year.

Corporate events & kickoffs

Kickoffs, company Christmas parties, vendor days, internal conferences. Professional coverage that respects the room — including events where discretion matters more than volume.

Product launches & press events

Launch moments, press walls, speaker arrivals. Same-day press selects available on request, so your announcement doesn't wait for the full gallery.

02 Your deliverables

What You Get,
and When

Edited gallery in up to 7 business days

A professionally edited, high-resolution gallery in up to 7 business days. Need a handful of images for press or LinkedIn on the day itself? Ask for same-day selects in the brief.

Full commercial usage rights

Every delivered image is licensed for commercial use — marketing, social, PR, internal comms, sponsor reports. No per-channel licensing fees.

On-site headshots & environmental portraits

A field-studio setup between sessions: employee headshots and environmental portraits shot on location, so your team gets updated profile photos without booking a studio.
