How a booking works

From the first call to the final gallery

Simple to book, planned around your goals, and you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We find out what the photos are for, and what success looks like for you.

    Your partTell us about the event.

  2. 02

    Options on a strategy call

    You get three coverage options, explained on a strategy call.

    Your partBring whoever decides and pick what fits.

  3. 03

    Agreement and kickoff

    Everything is agreed in writing. At kickoff we go through speakers, names and key moments.

    Your partSign and share your programme.

  4. 04

    Planning to the day

    We make a shot list from your programme and check the venue before the day.

    Your partTell us when plans change.

  5. 05

    Event day and delivery

    One contact on site, a crew sized to what we agreed, and the full gallery in up to 7 business days.

    Your partHost your event.

Consent and privacy

How we support your event's consent process

  • Your process, followed

    Tell us how attendees are informed, and we shoot to that.

  • No-photo signals honoured

    Wristbands, lanyards or stickers: brief us and we watch for them. Anyone who asks to be left out is left out.

  • You control what gets used

    Delivery goes to you first, in a private gallery, and images come out if someone raises a concern.
