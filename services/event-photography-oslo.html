01 Who this is for

Built for B2B and Public-Sector Organisers

Recap works with organisations, not consumers. The brief is usually the same one: document the whole programme, build a library the rest of the organisation can reuse, and keep the coordination overhead low on an already busy day.

Conference and summit organisers

Multi-track programmes where keynotes, breakouts and the sponsor floor all have to be covered inside one running schedule, without anything being missed because two things happened at once.

Marketing and communications teams

In-house teams who need press images, social content and internal comms assets out of a single event, shot with the end use in mind rather than handed over as an archive folder.

Event agencies and production partners

Agencies who need a local Oslo crew that reads a run-of-show, works alongside AV, venue and PR, and does not need supervising while you run the event.

Public, cultural and diplomatic programmes

Trade missions, receptions, public art projects and cultural programmes where discretion, protocol and fast press delivery matter more than volume.

02 Scope of coverage

What We Cover at Your Event

From 20-person seminars to multi-track programmes with several hundred attendees. If it has a schedule, speakers or a stand, it can be covered.

  • Conferences and summits: keynotes, panels, breakout sessions, audience and stage detail across the full running order.
  • Trade shows and exhibitions: stand photography for exhibitors, staff at work, visitor traffic and product detail your sales team can use all year.
  • Corporate kickoffs and product launches: launch moments, press walls, speaker arrivals, and the internal-culture images HR and comms ask for later.
  • Press and diplomatic events: receptions, signings and delegation programmes where the press images are needed before the news cycle moves on.
  • Public and cultural programmes: openings, installations and city-facing events documented for both press and archive use.
03 Before the day

How We Plan Your Coverage

Most of the risk in event photography is settled before anyone picks up a camera. Four steps, none of which need much of your time.

Step 01

Discovery call

Twenty minutes on the event: format, audience, what the images are for and which moments cannot be missed.

Step 02

Pre-event brief

A short written brief covering deliverables, delivery timing, usage and the people who need to be recognised on the day.

Step 03

Run-of-show and shot list

We work from your run-of-show and turn it into a shot list, so parallel sessions and set-piece moments are planned rather than chased.

Step 04

AV and venue coordination

Access, lighting, stage positions and any restrictions are agreed with your AV and venue contacts before the doors open.

06 Consent and privacy

How We Support Your Event's Consent Process

You own the consent process for your event. Our job is to work inside it and make it easy to honour on a busy day.

We follow your documented process

Before the event you tell us how attendees are informed: registration notices, signage, badge markers or a line in the programme. We shoot to that, and we ask rather than assume when something is unclear.

No-photo signals are honoured on the spot

If your event marks people who do not want to be photographed, with wristbands, lanyard colours or stickers, brief us and we watch for them. Anyone who asks to be left out is left out, no discussion needed.

You control what gets used

Delivery goes to you first, through a private gallery. If someone raises a concern after the event, tell us and the images come out of the gallery.
