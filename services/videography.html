01 What we produce

Event Video Your Team Will Actually Publish

Conference video production for B2B events across Oslo and beyond — from a single highlight reel to a full content package.

Aftermovies & brand films

A 2–4 minute branded film that captures what the event was actually about — including a promo cut that sells next year's tickets before the programme is even announced.

Highlight reels for social

30–90 second faster cuts for LinkedIn, Instagram and internal channels. Built to be posted, not archived.

Full event recordings

Complete session recordings — keynotes, panels and presentations, delivered ready to publish or share internally.

Vox pops & testimonials

Attendee vox pops and planned speaker or sponsor testimonials, recorded between sessions with a simple on-site interview setup.

02 Social-first video

One Filming Day,
Every Format

Vertical clips for LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok

Every filming day produces a 16:9 master edit and 9:16 vertical clips from the same material. Your social team gets platform-ready video — no awkward crops, no separate shoot.

Same-day edits

Need a clip published while the event is still running? Same-day edits are available when arranged in the brief.

A typical eight-hour filming day yields the main edit, a set of vertical teasers, and cutdowns sized for email and web — one shoot, weeks of publishing.

03 After the event

Content That Keeps Working After the Event

Most event footage gets delivered, posted once, and abandoned. We plan the edit around what your team will actually publish in the weeks and months after the day itself:

  • Speaker and session clips your marketing team can schedule for weeks
  • A 60–90 second promo film for next year's ticket and sponsor sales
  • Cutdowns sized for LinkedIn, email and your website
04 How it works

From Brief to Delivery

Step 1

Brief

We agree deliverables, key moments, formats and deadlines before the day. You get one contact from start to finish.

Step 2

Filming

We film discreetly around your programme — sessions, atmosphere, interviews — without staging the event.

Step 3

Edit

Editing, colour and sound, plus your branding: logos, lower-thirds and subtitles in Norwegian or English.

Step 4

Delivery

High-resolution MP4s via private link. Highlight reels typically within a week; aftermovies in up to 14 business days.
