How a booking works

From the first call to the final delivery

Simple to book, planned around your goals, and you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We find out what the photos and video are for, and what success looks like for you.

    Your partTell us about the event.

  2. 02

    Options on a strategy call

    You get two or three coverage options, explained on a strategy call.

    Your partBring whoever decides and pick what fits.

  3. 03

    Agreement and kickoff

    One booking covers photos and video, all agreed in writing. At kickoff we go through speakers, rooms and key moments.

    Your partSign and share your programme.

  4. 04

    Planning to the day

    We make a shot list for each room we cover and check the venue before the day.

    Your partTell us when plans change.

  5. 05

    Event days

    One contact on site, and a crew sized to what we agreed.

    Your partHost your event.

  6. 06

    Delivery and review

    Photos in up to 7 business days. Video in up to 14 business days, with two revision rounds.

    Your partName one person to collect feedback.

Consent and privacy

How we support your event's consent process

  • Your process, followed

    Tell us how your attendees are informed about photos and filming, and the crew follows your process on the day.

  • Opt-out signals honoured

    Wristbands, lanyards or stickers: brief us and the whole crew watches for them. Anyone who asks to be left out is left out.

  • You control what gets used

    Delivery goes to you first, privately, and material comes out if someone raises a concern.
