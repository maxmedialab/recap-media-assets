Event Photography and Video in Oslo

Recap Media covers B2B events across Oslo and beyond. Maks Strzepka leads every shoot with a vetted crew sized to the event, so photo and video sit under one booking with one point of contact. Everything is shot with the end use in mind, so the material keeps working long after the doors close.
