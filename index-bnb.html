Photo and Video

02 How it works

A Clear Plan, Then a Clean Delivery

Step 1

Tell us about your event

You reach out and get a real reply within a business day: availability and a straight quote. We ask a few questions up front so the quote reflects the actual job and the scope is spelled out.

Step 2

We write the brief, you sign it off

Before the day, you get a written brief — what we'll capture, in what order, and why — built from your schedule, your priorities and your must-have shots. You confirm it or correct it — so nothing on the day is a surprise.

Step 3

One point of contact on the day

The person who planned your brief is the one behind the camera. You're never handed off to a stranger. We cover the day to the plan — photo and video — and bring in vetted Oslo crew when an event needs more hands.

Step 4

Delivery you can actually use

Same-day photo selects while the event is still fresh, then edited photo and video sized for the platforms you'll post on. Delivered on the date we agreed.
