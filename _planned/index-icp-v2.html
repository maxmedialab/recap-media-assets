01 What you get from the day

From one event
to months of content

One day of filming can fund months of content if you plan it that way from the start. You choose how much of it to hand off — pick what fits your team's bandwidth.

  • Your footage, ready to use. One crew captures your event; you receive organized, finished footage — no months of raw-file sorting.
  • Your content series, planned before filming starts. We map the full series up front — titles, formats, timing — and handle the editing.
  • Your content, published on schedule. We handle timing, platforms, and publishing — hours of coordination become minutes of approval.
How it works

From brief to content engine in four steps

Step 1

Brief

A short call before the event — what you're running, who it's for, what the content needs to do afterwards.

Step 2

Capture

We cover the day — photo and video, one crew, one point of contact.

Step 3

Build

Within days you get the edited gallery and films, plus the content plan: what gets published, where, and when.

Step 4 (Optional)

Run

We schedule and publish it for you — you just approve. Or take the plan and run it in-house.
