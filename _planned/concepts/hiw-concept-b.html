01 How it works

You run the event.
We run the production.

Your event moves through five stages, from the first conversation to content that keeps working months later. Your part stays small at every one.

Stage 1 of 5 · Before anything is booked

Brief

You get clear on your event, your audience, and what the content needs to achieve once the day is over. We ask the questions, you talk about your event.

Your partOne conversation.

Leads to: one shared picture of what a good result looks like.

Stage 2 of 5 · Before the day

Plan

You see the content plan before anything is filmed: what gets made, where it goes, and when. Rooms, moments and timings are mapped in advance.

Your partApprove the plan.

Leads to: nothing left to decide while the event is running.

Stage 3 of 5 · On the day

Capture

One coordinated crew covers the day for you: photo, video and content production under a single point of contact, working to the plan you approved.

Your partHost your event.

Leads to: the day fully covered while you stay with your guests.

Stage 4 of 5 · After the day

Build

You get finished content, ready to use. One event day becomes photos, film and pieces built for the places you publish, all from the same coverage.

Your partPick your favourites.

Leads to: one day, many outputs, all on brief.

Stage 5 of 5 · Optional · The months after

Run

You keep the content moving for months. We schedule and publish it for you, or hand you the plan so your team can run it in house.

Your partApprove each batch.

Leads to: your event still earning attention long after it ends.
