Conference Photography and Video in Oslo

Conference photography and video in Oslo from one crew, with one booking and one point of contact. Edited gallery in up to 7 business days, video in up to 14, full commercial rights.

01 Photography & Video

What We Photograph & Film

One crew photographs and films the whole programme, parallel tracks included. Priority sessions are agreed in the brief and the crew is sized to the number of rooms, so a multi-track conference does not need separate photo and video vendors working alongside each other.

Keynotes & Panel Sessions

Speakers at the podium, on stage, during panel discussions. Audience reactions. Footage that represents your speakers well, so it is material they are happy to share with their own networks.

Sponsor Documentation

Booth activity, product demonstrations, sponsor interactions. Your sponsors get proof of presence and engagement: material for their follow-up in the weeks after the event, and a reason to come back next year.

Networking & Breaks

The coffee breaks, the hallway conversations, the energy between sessions. Images that show what attending feels like, which is exactly what promotes next year's edition.

Delegate Experience

The room, the setup, the flow. Photography that captures the conference itself: how it looked, how it felt, who was in the room.

02 Your deliverables

What You Get,
and When

Edited photo gallery in up to 7 business days

High-resolution, professionally edited images in up to 7 business days, licensed for full commercial use with no per-channel licensing fees. Press-ready photos on the day itself, or within 24 hours, can be arranged in advance when a press office, sponsor or newsroom deadline needs them. The full gallery follows within the standard window.

Highlight film, session recordings and social clips

One provider delivers both the highlight film and the full session recordings, cut from the same shoot. The highlight film is typically around 2 minutes, depending on the event, session recordings cover the talks you select, and vertical 9:16 clips are cut for LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok. Standard video delivery is up to 14 business days, and faster highlight cuts can be arranged in advance in the brief.

One consistent look across everything

Photography and video shot to a single brief and edited together. Consistent tone, framing and visual language. No mismatched deliverables, no gaps.

03 How It Works

From Brief to Delivery

📋

One brief

You tell us the conference structure, key speakers, sponsor needs, and what matters for marketing afterwards. We build a shot list from your priorities. Organisers based outside Norway brief us the same way, over email and video calls.

🎥

One booking, one point of contact

Maks leads every shoot with a vetted crew sized to the event, working to your brief. Coordinated, discreet, consistent all day. For multi-track conferences, additional crew is arranged under the same booking.

✓

Delivery & done

Gallery in up to 7 business days, video in up to 14. One contact handles questions, changes and coordination through it all.

One booking

Why Booking Photo & Video Together Matters

One crew can handle both photography and video at a conference. Recap Media shoots and edits both to a single brief, so you book conference photography and video in Oslo once rather than hiring a photographer and a video team who have never worked the same room.

Booked Separately

Separate briefs, separate coverage plans, separate deliveries, and no guarantee the visual look matches. You juggle two vendors.

One Booking

One brief and one point of contact, so the photography and the video work together instead of arriving as two unrelated sets of files.

On the Day Itself

A single shot list covering the keynote, the sponsor floor and the breaks. No two vendors negotiating the same space in front of your speakers.

Proof

Three Rooms, Two Days, 46 Speakers

Flutter Vikings 2022 in Oslo ran 46 speakers across three parallel rooms over two days, photographed and filmed under one booking. Photography covered the keynotes and panels in every room, the sponsor booths and the conversations in between. On the video side, roughly 20 hours of footage became 43 published videos.
