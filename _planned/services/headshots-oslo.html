Professional Headshots in Oslo

Team portrait days at your office, individual sessions and headshot stations at corporate events. Edited photos in 3–5 days, ready for LinkedIn and company use.

01 Team portraits

Consistent Headshots Across Your Team

A morning or afternoon at your office. Everyone gets a set of consistent, professional portraits shot on neutral backgrounds — whether that's five people or fifty.

On-location setup

We bring lighting and backdrop to your office. No studio visits needed — your team stays in their normal environment and just needs a quiet room while we shoot.

Individual portraits

Each person gets a set of edited selects. Neutral grey or white background so headshots look uniform across your team. Style — serious, approachable or candid — is discussed in advance.

Fast delivery

Edited gallery delivered within 3–5 business days. High-resolution JPEGs ready for LinkedIn profiles, email signatures, company websites and printed materials.

Full commercial use

Full commercial usage rights are included. Use headshots across your website, social media, internal communications and marketing — no per-channel fees or licensing restrictions.

02 At your event

Headshots Between Sessions

Running a conference or corporate gathering? A headshot station works as an add-on to event photography. We set up a field studio in a breakout room or quiet corner and photograph attendees or staff in parallel with main coverage.

Common uses: attendee networking photos for LinkedIn, updated staff portraits during kickoffs, speaker headshots at conferences. People drop by when there's a gap in the programme, and each portrait only takes a few minutes, so nobody misses a session. Same setup, same 3–5 day turnaround, and it runs alongside photography or videography on the day.

03 Solo portraits

One-on-One Headshot Sessions

Need a fresh LinkedIn photo or updated CV headshot? Book an individual session.

  • Same setup and editing as our team days — an individual session with a set of edited selects, shot on a neutral background, booked for one person
  • Shot where it suits you — your office, a venue in Oslo and beyond, or a location of your choice
  • Scheduled around you — we coordinate a time that suits your schedule
  • Edited and delivered in 3–5 business days
  • Full usage rights for LinkedIn, CV, web and social
04 How it works

From Brief to Delivery

Step 1

Brief

Tell us how many people, preferred date and location. We agree on background, style and delivery format.

Step 2

Setup

We arrive early to set up lighting and backdrop. Check the space, adjust for natural light and any branding elements you want visible.

Step 3

Shoot

Each person sits for their portraits. Quick and straightforward — we handle lighting, framing and direction, so nobody needs to know how to pose. Most people are done in under ten minutes.

Step 4

Delivery

Edited gallery within 3–5 business days. High-resolution JPEGs via a private online gallery, ready to download and use.
