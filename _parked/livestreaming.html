01 What we stream

Live Streaming for B2B Events

Live streaming for conferences, corporate meetings and hybrid events across Greater Oslo — in Norwegian, English, or both.

Conferences & seminars

Single sessions or full programmes streamed live to remote attendees — and parallel tracks streamed simultaneously when the programme calls for it.

AGMs & board meetings

Annual general meetings, board meetings and quarterly presentations — including private, access-controlled webcasts for shareholders and stakeholders.

Hybrid events

An in-person audience plus a live remote one, with Q&A, chat and polls — so the people at home are participants, not just viewers.

Product launches & panel debates

Announcements and debates streamed where your audience already is — public platforms or an embedded player on your own site.

02 How it works

Planned Before the First Frame Goes Live

Step 1

Technical brief & connectivity check

Before the event we map the venue: internet capacity, camera positions, audio feeds from the AV desk, and the platforms you want to reach. You get a clear run-of-show, not surprises.

Step 2

Live production

Multi-camera coverage with speaker slides mixed live — picture-in-picture, side-by-side or full-screen — and remote speakers patched into the programme.

Step 3

Redundancy

We stream on venue broadband and run a bonded mobile connection alongside it for critical streams, with a local recording as the safety net.

03 Platforms & interactive features

Streamed Where Your
Audience Already Is

Public reach or restricted access — the destination is a decision in the technical brief, not a limitation.

  • YouTube Live, Vimeo, Microsoft Teams, Zoom — or an embedded player on a branded page
  • Multi-platform streaming to several destinations at once
  • Q&A, live chat and polls for hybrid audiences
  • Password-protected and domain-restricted streams for AGMs and internal events
  • Remote and international speakers patched into the live programme
04 After the broadcast

Your Stream Becomes On-Demand Content

A stream shouldn't end when the broadcast does. Every stream is recorded in high quality, so the event keeps reaching people who never logged in live:

  • Full recording delivered as MP4 — ready for your website, intranet or YouTube channel
  • Bite-size clips cut from the recording for social and email
  • A session archive that promotes next year's event all year
More than streaming

One Booking for Live Streaming, Video and Photography

Streaming specialists stop at the stream. We cover the live broadcast, the event photography and the edited video under one booking and one technical brief — no juggling a streaming provider and a separate photo crew on the day.
