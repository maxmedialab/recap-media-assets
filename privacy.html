01 Introduction

MAXFILMS STRZEPKA, trading as Recap Media (org. no. 928 325 261), is the data controller responsible for your personal data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently and in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Norwegian law.

This privacy policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it. If you have any questions, contact us at booking@recapmedia.no.

02 Data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact and identity data: First name, last name, email address, phone number, organisation name
  • Enquiry data: What you tell us in the quote form or when you book a call (event date, details about your event)
  • Communication data: Records of your communications with us (emails, calls, messages)
  • Technical data: IP address and browser type, which every website and its security service receive; pages visited and time spent on the site, only if you accept analytics cookies (see section 10)
  • Transaction data: Invoice information, payment records (for contracted clients)
  • Images: Photos and video of people at the events we cover, and portraits we take for clients (see section 7)

03 How we collect your data

We collect data through:

  • The quote request form and the call booking calendar on this website
  • Direct communications (email, phone, WhatsApp)
  • VidLead Studio, our website and CRM platform (built on GoHighLevel), when you submit a form or book a call
  • Cookies and similar technologies on this website (see section 10)
  • Contractual documentation (for booked clients)

04 How we use your data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries: Processing your quote requests and answering questions (legal basis: legitimate interest / contract performance)
  • To provide services: Delivering the photography and video services you have booked (legal basis: contract performance)
  • To manage client relationships: Sending invoices, project updates and follow-up communication (legal basis: contract performance / legitimate interest)
  • To improve our website: Analysing how visitors use the site and where enquiries come from (legal basis: consent, given through the cookie banner)
  • To comply with legal obligations: Retaining financial records as required by Norwegian law (legal basis: legal obligation)

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

05 Marketing communications

We may send you occasional email updates about our services if you have given explicit consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by clicking "Unsubscribe" in any marketing email or contacting us directly at booking@recapmedia.no.

06 Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your data with:

  • Service providers: VidLead Studio (website, forms, CRM and call booking), Google (Google Workspace for email and files; Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager) and Cloudflare (website security and performance), all acting as data processors under our instructions. Section 10 says where they process data.
  • Crew and subcontractors: Trusted photographers and videographers who work with us on specific events (limited to what is necessary for the project)
  • Legal authorities: Where required by law or in response to a valid legal request

07 People in the photos and video we take

When a client books us, we photograph and film their event, or portraits of their team, on the client's behalf; the client decides how the material is used and tells attendees about it. We also use selected photos and video in our own portfolio and marketing, unless the client has asked us not to (legal basis: legitimate interest). For corporate headshots, we only use portraits in our portfolio with the client's permission.

If you appear in our material and want it removed from our portfolio and marketing, including our website and social media, email booking@recapmedia.no. We take it down and leave it out from then on.

08 Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy:

  • Enquiry data: 2 years from the date of enquiry if no contract is formed
  • Client data and project records: 5 years from the end of the project (in line with Norwegian accounting law)
  • Marketing consent records: Until consent is withdrawn, plus 1 year
  • Website analytics data: As specified by the analytics provider's data retention settings
  • Photos and video we take for clients: raw files are kept for at least 6 months after delivery, or longer if agreed with the client; material we use in our portfolio is kept until you ask us to remove it (see section 7)

09 Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Right to restriction: Ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interest, including direct marketing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at booking@recapmedia.no. We will respond within 30 days. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with Datatilsynet (the Norwegian Data Protection Authority) at www.datatilsynet.no.

10 Cookies

This site uses cookies and similar storage technologies. The first time you visit, a banner asks which categories you're OK with. You can change your mind any time by clearing cookies for this site or using the customise option the banner provides.

Essential

Most are required for the site to work, so they are set automatically and you can't turn them off. They handle session management, form security, protection against automated traffic and remembering your consent choice. Some session and attribution identifiers set by our website platform (VidLead Studio) are also included here: the platform sets them regardless of consent, so we list them as essential to reflect how they actually behave.

Analytics (opt-in)

Help us see what's working on the site: which pages get read, where visitors come from, what they interact with. We use Google Analytics 4, served through Google Tag Manager. Only set if you consent.

Advertising (opt-in)

Let us attribute quote requests to specific traffic sources or outreach channels, using VidLead Studio's visitor attribution system. Helps us understand which outreach actually connects with people. Only set if you consent, apart from the platform identifiers listed under Essential.

Third parties involved

Some cookies and requests come from services we use, not directly from us:

  • Google (Google Workspace, Google Analytics 4 / Google Tag Manager): Email, files and analytics processor. Data may be transferred to Google servers including in the US, under Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • VidLead Studio: Our website and CRM platform. Handles session, form submission and attribution data. VidLead's servers and sub-processors are located in the United States. For full details, see VidLead's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: Protects the site against automated traffic and measures how fast pages load. It sets one short-lived security cookie and processes connection data, including your IP address, on servers in many countries, including the US.
  • Fonts and site files: These load from content delivery networks (jsDelivr, Google Fonts, Fontshare and Bunny Fonts), which receive your IP address in order to send them.

Managing your consent

Clear cookies for this site in your browser to be asked again on your next visit. You can reject all non-essential cookies through the banner, and the site works either way. For a full breakdown of every cookie name, purpose and duration, the customise option in the banner shows the complete list.

11 Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, with the "Last updated" date shown at the top. We will notify contracted clients of material changes by email.

Questions about your data?

Email or call us to ask what we hold about you, or to use any of your rights.
